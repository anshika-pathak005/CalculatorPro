# CalculatorPro

A student-friendly mathematics toolkit built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

CalculatorPro was created to help school students quickly verify their calculations and understand mathematical concepts through simple, easy-to-use calculators. The idea originated from observing students in classes 6–7 who often solved questions manually but wanted a reliable way to confirm their answers.

The project is designed to be modular and scalable, allowing new calculators to be added as students' needs evolve.

- Weight Converter

- Time Converter

---

## Why I Built This

Many students solve mathematics questions manually and then look for a quick way to verify whether their answers are correct. Instead of using complex calculators or searching online repeatedly, I wanted to create a single platform where students could:

- Check their answers instantly.
- Understand calculations step by step.
- Practice mathematics more confidently.
- Access multiple calculators from one place.

CalculatorPro is intended to grow continuously based on real feedback from students.
