Exponents & Powers

Solve exponent operations ($a^m + b^n$, $a^m \times a^n$, laws of indices), scientific notation, and formulas!

Second Term Parameters (b and n):

Product Law: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ

When multiplying powers with the same base, add their exponents.

Quotient Law: aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ

When dividing powers with the same base, subtract exponent of denominator.

Power of a Power: (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐˣⁿ

To raise a power to a power, multiply the exponents.

Zero Exponent: a⁰ = 1

Any non-zero base raised to power 0 equals 1.

Negative Exponent: a⁻ⁿ = 1 / aⁿ

A negative exponent is equivalent to reciprocal with positive exponent.

Power of a Product / Quotient: (ab)ⁿ = aⁿbⁿ , (a/b)ⁿ = aⁿ/bⁿ

Power applies individually to both factors in multiplication or division.
