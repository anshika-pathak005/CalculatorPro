Profit & Loss Calculator

Calculate profit, loss, selling price, cost price, and percentages.

Profit
Profit = SP - CP
(When SP > CP)
Loss
Loss = CP - SP
(When CP > SP)
Profit Percentage
Profit% = (Profit ÷ CP) × 100
Loss Percentage
Loss% = (Loss ÷ CP) × 100
Find SP (Profit)
SP = CP × (100 + P%) ÷ 100
Find SP (Loss)
SP = CP × (100 - L%) ÷ 100
Find CP (Profit)
CP = SP × 100 ÷ (100 + P%)
Find CP (Loss)
CP = SP × 100 ÷ (100 - L%)
